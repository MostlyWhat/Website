## About the Role

We're seeking a talented UI/UX Designer to shape the visual identity and user experience of our products. You'll work closely with developers and stakeholders to create beautiful, functional designs.

## What You'll Do

- Design user interfaces for web applications from concept to final handoff
- Maintain and evolve our design system in Figma
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create prototypes and user flows
- Collaborate with developers to ensure design fidelity

## Requirements

- 4+ years of UI/UX design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating web and mobile design work
- Proficiency in Figma and prototyping tools
- Understanding of design systems and component-based design
- Experience with user research methodologies
- Excellent visual design skills and attention to detail

## Nice to Have

- Experience with motion design and micro-interactions
- Knowledge of HTML/CSS
- Experience designing for accessibility
- Background in branding or graphic design

## What We Offer

- Competitive salary and equity
- Remote-first culture with flexible hours
- Annual learning budget ($2,000/year)
- Latest equipment of your choice
- 4 weeks paid time off
