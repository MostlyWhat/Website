## Response Time Overview

We're committed to providing timely support for all your needs. Here's what you can expect.

## Standard Response Times

| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Critical | Within 2 hours | Within 8 hours |
| High | Within 4 hours | Within 24 hours |
| Normal | Within 24 hours | Within 72 hours |
| Low | Within 48 hours | Within 1 week |

## Business Hours

Our standard support hours are:

- **Monday - Friday:** 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (ICT/UTC+7)
- **Saturday - Sunday:** Limited monitoring

For critical issues outside business hours, use our urgent email: urgent@mostlywhat.com

## Priority Definitions

### Critical
- Production site is completely down
- Security breach or vulnerability
- Data loss or corruption
- Complete loss of critical functionality

### High
- Major feature is non-functional
- Significant performance degradation
- Issues affecting multiple users
- Blocking business operations

### Normal
- Feature not working as expected
- Non-critical bugs
- Questions about functionality
- Change requests

### Low
- Visual/cosmetic issues
- Feature requests
- General questions
- Documentation updates

## Escalation Process

If you feel your issue isn't being addressed appropriately:

1. Reply to your existing ticket with escalation request
2. Email our lead at team@mostlywhat.com
3. For clients with SLA agreements, refer to your contract

## Improving Response Times

Help us help you faster:

- Provide complete information upfront
- Include screenshots and error messages
- Respond promptly to our questions
- Use the appropriate priority level
