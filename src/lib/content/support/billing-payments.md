## Billing Overview

Information about how we handle payments, invoicing, and billing.

## Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

### Direct Transfer
- Bank transfer (preferred)
- Wire transfer for international clients
- Details provided on invoices

### Online Payments
- PayPal
- Wise (TransferWise)
- Stripe (for card payments)

### Cryptocurrency
- Available upon request
- BTC, ETH, USDT accepted

## Payment Schedule

### Project Payments
Our standard payment structure:

1. **30% Deposit** - Upon project agreement
2. **40% Milestone** - At design approval
3. **30% Final** - Upon project completion

For smaller projects (<$5,000), we may offer:
- 50% upfront, 50% on completion

### Maintenance & Retainer
- Monthly invoicing
- Due within 14 days of invoice
- Auto-payment options available

## Invoicing

### Invoice Details
Each invoice includes:
- Itemized services
- Project milestones completed
- Hours worked (if applicable)
- Payment instructions
- Due date

### Invoice Delivery
- Sent via email (PDF)
- Available in client portal
- Reminders sent 7 days before due

## Late Payments

We understand circumstances vary. Our policy:

- **Grace Period:** 7 days after due date
- **Late Fee:** 2% per month after grace period
- **Work Pause:** After 30 days overdue
- **Collections:** After 90 days overdue

Having trouble paying? Contact us before the due date to discuss options.

## Refund Policy

### Before Work Begins
- 100% refund of deposit minus 10% admin fee

### During Design Phase
- Partial refund based on work completed
- Design assets delivered

### During Development
- No refund for completed work
- Partial refund for incomplete work
- Source code delivered

### After Completion
- No refunds for completed projects
- Warranty coverage applies
