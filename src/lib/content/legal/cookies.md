# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: January 2024**

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

We use cookies and similar technologies including:
- **Cookies:** Small text files stored in your browser
- **Local Storage:** Browser-based storage for site preferences
- **Session Storage:** Temporary storage cleared when you close your browser

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core features like:
- Page navigation
- Secure area access
- Session management
- Form submission

**Duration:** Session to 1 year  
**Can be disabled:** No (required for site functionality)

### Analytics Cookies
We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our content and user experience.

Information collected includes:
- Pages visited and navigation paths
- Time spent on pages
- Device and browser information
- Geographic region (anonymized)
- Referral sources

**Provider:** Cloudflare Analytics  
**Duration:** Up to 2 years  
**Can be disabled:** Yes

### Preference Cookies
These cookies remember your choices and settings, such as:
- Language preference
- Theme settings (dark/light mode)
- Cookie consent choices

**Duration:** 1 year  
**Can be disabled:** Yes

### Marketing Cookies
We may use marketing cookies to:
- Deliver relevant content
- Measure advertising effectiveness
- Limit ad frequency

We do not currently run advertising on our site, but may implement marketing cookies in the future with proper notice.

## Managing Cookies

### Browser Settings
You can control cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies

### Cookie Preferences
You can manage your cookie preferences using our cookie settings panel. Click the "Cookie Settings" link in our footer to:
- View active cookies
- Enable/disable optional cookies
- Withdraw consent

### Opt-Out Links
- Google Analytics: [tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- Network Advertising Initiative: [optout.networkadvertising.org](https://optout.networkadvertising.org)

## Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect your experience:

| Cookie Type | Impact if Disabled |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Essential | Site may not function correctly |
| Analytics | We cannot improve based on your usage |
| Preference | Settings won't be remembered |
| Marketing | Less relevant content and ads |

## Do Not Track

Our website respects the "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser setting. When DNT is enabled, we will not set non-essential cookies.
