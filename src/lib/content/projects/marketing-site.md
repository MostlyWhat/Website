# B2B SaaS Marketing Website

A conversion-optimized marketing website designed to drive signups and demonstrate product value.

## The Challenge

The client needed a website that could effectively communicate their complex B2B product while driving qualified leads through the sales funnel.

## Our Solution

We created a comprehensive marketing platform:

- **Dynamic content** powered by Contentful CMS
- **A/B testing framework** for continuous optimization
- **Lead scoring** integration with HubSpot
- **Interactive demos** showcasing product features
- **Case study system** with filtering and search

## Implementation Details

- SvelteKit with static generation for speed
- Contentful for flexible content management
- Segment for analytics and data routing
- PostHog for product analytics and A/B tests

## Results

- **45% increase** in qualified leads
- **28% improvement** in time on page
- **3x more** demo requests
