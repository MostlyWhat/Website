# Headless E-commerce Platform

A blazing-fast e-commerce storefront built with headless architecture for a premium retail brand.

## The Challenge

The client's existing platform was slow, inflexible, and couldn't support their rapid growth. They needed a modern solution that could handle high traffic while providing an exceptional shopping experience.

## Our Solution

We implemented a headless commerce architecture:

- **Headless Shopify** for inventory and order management
- **Custom SvelteKit frontend** for blazing performance
- **Contentful CMS** for marketing content
- **Stripe integration** for seamless payments
- **Edge-optimized** for global performance

## Key Features

- Product search with Algolia
- Dynamic pricing and promotions
- Multi-currency support
- Guest checkout optimization
- Abandoned cart recovery

## Results

- **92 Lighthouse score** (up from 45)
- **35% increase** in conversion rate
- **2.5x faster** page load times
