# Brand & Identity

Your brand is more than a logo—it's every interaction someone has with your business. We create cohesive identity systems that build recognition and trust.

## Our Process

### Brand Discovery

Before designing anything, we understand:

- Your mission, vision, and values
- Target audience and their preferences
- Competitive positioning
- Brand personality and voice

### Visual Identity Development

We create a complete visual system including:

- **Logo Design**: Primary logo and variations for different contexts
- **Color Palette**: Primary, secondary, and accent colors with accessibility in mind
- **Typography**: Font selections for headlines, body, and UI
- **Iconography**: Custom icon sets that reinforce brand identity
- **Photography Style**: Guidelines for imagery selection and treatment

### Design System Creation

For digital products, we build comprehensive design systems:

- Component library with all UI elements
- Design tokens for consistent styling
- Usage guidelines and best practices
- Accessibility specifications

## Deliverables

### Brand Guidelines

A comprehensive document covering:

- Logo usage and clear space rules
- Color specifications (RGB, CMYK, Pantone)
- Typography scales and hierarchy
- Photography and illustration guidelines
- Voice and tone guidelines

### Digital Assets

Production-ready files including:

- Logo files in all formats (SVG, PNG, EPS)
- Color palette files for design tools
- Font files and licenses
- Template library for common applications

## Our Philosophy

We believe in:

- **Simplicity**: Memorable brands are simple brands
- **Consistency**: Every touchpoint reinforces the brand
- **Flexibility**: Systems that work across all contexts
- **Timelessness**: Design that ages gracefully
