## About the Role

We're looking for a Senior Frontend Developer to join our engineering team. You'll work on cutting-edge web applications using modern technologies like SvelteKit, TypeScript, and TailwindCSS.

## What You'll Do

- Build and maintain responsive, accessible web applications
- Collaborate with designers to implement pixel-perfect UIs
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code
- Mentor junior developers and contribute to technical decisions
- Optimize applications for performance and scalability

## Requirements

- 5+ years of frontend development experience
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript and modern JavaScript
- Experience with Svelte/SvelteKit or React
- Deep understanding of CSS, including Tailwind or similar utility frameworks
- Experience with testing frameworks (Vitest, Playwright, etc.)
- Excellent communication skills in English

## Nice to Have

- Experience with serverless architectures (Cloudflare Workers)
- Contributions to open source projects
- Experience with design systems
- Knowledge of accessibility standards (WCAG)

## What We Offer

- Competitive salary and equity
- Remote-first culture with flexible hours
- Annual learning budget ($2,000/year)
- Latest equipment of your choice
- 4 weeks paid time off
