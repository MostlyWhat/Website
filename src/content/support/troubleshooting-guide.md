## Guided Troubleshooter

Use this guide to diagnose common issues before contacting support.

## Website Issues

### The site won't load

**Step 1: Check if it's just you**
- Visit [downforeveryoneorjustme.com](https://downforeveryoneorjustme.com)
- If it's down for everyone, it's a server issue

**Step 2: Clear your DNS cache**
- Windows: `ipconfig /flushdns`
- Mac: `sudo dscacheutil -flushcache`

**Step 3: Try a different network**
- Switch from WiFi to mobile data
- Use a VPN

### The site is slow

**Step 1: Test your connection**
- Run a speed test at [fast.com](https://fast.com)
- Try loading other websites

**Step 2: Disable extensions**
- Open an incognito/private window
- Check if performance improves

**Step 3: Clear browser cache**
- Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
- Clear cached images and files

### Forms aren't submitting

**Step 1: Check required fields**
- Look for red error messages
- Ensure all required fields are filled

**Step 2: Check your email address**
- Verify it's correctly formatted
- No spaces before or after

**Step 3: Disable ad blockers**
- They may block form submissions

## Account Issues

### Can't log in

**Step 1: Reset your password**
- Use the "Forgot Password" link
- Check spam folder for reset email

**Step 2: Clear cookies**
- Settings > Privacy > Clear Cookies
- Try logging in again

**Step 3: Check account status**
- Contact us to verify account is active

### Not receiving emails

**Step 1: Check spam/junk folder**
- Move emails to inbox
- Mark as "Not Spam"

**Step 2: Whitelist our domain**
- Add @mostlywhat.com to contacts
- Add to safe senders list

**Step 3: Verify email address**
- Ensure email is correct in account

## Still Need Help?

If these steps don't resolve your issue:

1. Note what you've tried
2. [Submit a ticket](/support#ticket-form)
3. Include all troubleshooting steps attempted

We'll get back to you within 24 hours.
