## Welcome to MostlyWhat

Thank you for choosing MostlyWhat for your project. This guide will help you get started and make the most of our collaboration.

## Initial Consultation

After you reach out to us, we'll schedule an initial consultation to:

- Understand your project requirements
- Discuss timelines and milestones
- Review your budget and scope
- Answer any questions you have

## Project Kickoff

Once we've agreed on the scope, we'll begin with:

1. **Discovery Phase** - Deep dive into your requirements
2. **Design Phase** - Create wireframes and mockups
3. **Development Phase** - Build your solution
4. **Testing Phase** - Ensure quality and performance
5. **Launch Phase** - Deploy and go live

## Communication Channels

We use the following channels for communication:

- **Email** - For formal communications and documentation
- **Slack/Discord** - For quick questions and updates (if preferred)
- **Video Calls** - For detailed discussions and reviews

## What to Prepare

Before our kickoff meeting, please prepare:

- Brand guidelines (logos, colors, fonts)
- Content and copy (text, images, videos)
- Access credentials (hosting, domains, etc.)
- Reference sites you like
